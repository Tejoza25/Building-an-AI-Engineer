## 🎯 Objective

# 📖 Overview

Today's learning focused on two of the most widely used prompting techniques in modern AI applications: **Few-shot Prompting** and **Chain-of-Thought Prompting**.

Rather than relying on simple instructions, these techniques help guide an LLM toward producing more accurate, structured, and context-aware responses.

By experimenting with different prompt styles, I observed how prompt design directly affects the reasoning process and the overall quality of generated outputs.

---

# 🧠 Concepts Learned

## 1. Few-shot Prompting

Few-shot Prompting involves providing the AI model with a small number of input-output examples before presenting the actual task.

These examples help the model recognize the desired pattern, response style, and expected format.

### Benefits

- Improves response consistency
- Reduces ambiguity
- Helps the model understand complex tasks
- Produces more structured outputs

---

## 2. Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting

Chain-of-Thought Prompting encourages the model to reason through a problem step by step before generating the final answer.

Instead of producing an immediate response, the model follows a logical reasoning process, which often leads to more accurate solutions for complex tasks.

### Benefits

- Better logical reasoning
- Improved problem solving
- Higher accuracy on multi-step tasks
- Increased transparency of the reasoning process

---

# 🔬 Practical Exercise

As part of today's task, I compared responses generated using three different prompting approaches:

### Standard Prompt

A direct instruction without examples or reasoning guidance.

**Observation**

- Quick responses
- Less structured
- More likely to miss important details

---

### Few-shot Prompt

Provided multiple examples before asking the actual question.

**Observation**

- More consistent formatting
- Better understanding of the required output
- Improved response quality

---

### Chain-of-Thought Prompt

Requested the model to reason step by step before producing the final answer.

**Observation**

- More logical explanations
- Better handling of complex problems
- Higher quality reasoning

---

# 📊 Comparison Summary

| Prompting Technique | Strength |
|--------------------|----------|
| Standard Prompt | Fast responses for simple tasks |
| Few-shot Prompting | Improves consistency and output formatting |
| Chain-of-Thought Prompting | Enhances reasoning and multi-step problem solving |

# NVIDIA Corporation — Form 10-K Excerpt (FY2024 - FY2025)

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)

### Consolidated Revenue Summary

NVIDIA reported record revenue across its core compute and networking architectures. Revenue growth was heavily driven by hyperscale cloud demand for accelerated computing platforms.

### Data Center Operations & Growth

Data Center segment revenue surged primarily due to the adoption of the NVIDIA Hopper GPU architecture (including H100 and H200 systems) and early deployments of the Blackwell platform. Enterprise customers, cloud service providers (CSPs), and sovereign AI initiatives accounted for the primary revenue growth in accelerated computing clusters.

### Risk Factors & Supply Chain Disclosures

The company is dependent on third-party foundries, primarily Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC), for wafer fabrication, advanced packaging (CoWoS), and testing. Any capacity constraint, geopolitical disruption, or manufacturing yield degradation could materially impact shipment volumes of Blackwell architecture components.

Additionally, export restrictions imposed by the U.S. government on advanced computing semiconductors to certain geographic regions, including China, have limited addressable market size for high-density AI accelerators.
